# Study: RS in-game sprites — character design (hair, costume, colour scheme) + cast / activation poses

- source: 16 user-provided RS in-game sprites (11 from study 008 + front ×2, cast ×1, activation ×2), 8× WebP → 48×48 via `.claude/skills/rs-style-sprite/scripts/rs_ref.py`; colour schemes measured with `rs_check.py` (`scheme`). Details: `.claude/skills/rs-style-sprite/references/design-v3.md`
- true size: front 29–30 h × 16 w; cast 30 h (32 with staff); activation 30 h × 24 w (35 with spell FX) | colors: 16–35

## Colour scheme
- pick one: analogous (hair ≈ costume hue, 10/16) + a 3–8% opposite accent; complementary (≥120°, 3/16); contrast (60–90°, 3/16)
- hair is the single biggest colour mass (45–80% of the head area); warm colours appear in every design (10/16 are warm-dominant); gold/yellow is pushed to white for sparkle
- dark costumes start their ramp at a tinted dark (#1c2b34-ish) so they don't merge with the black outline

## Construction
- hair: 2–4 px clumps with 1 px tips poking out; per clump dark rim → mid → light streak → white dot; 3–4 bangs; head width 0.9–1.4× body; at least one moving piece (ponytail, side tail, ribbon, headband tail, sash, scarf) and one hair ornament in the accent colour
- torso split by 3 horizontal bands: bright neck piece → chest band → 1-row belt in the accent; sleeves end with a coloured cuff; skirt flares to 12–16 px with a 1-row hem trim; tiny 2–3 px shoes in a colour different from the costume
- staff taller than the body (2–4 px above the head, 1–2 below the feet)

## Poses
- cast: upright, head down 1, **eyes closed = 2 px horizontal black dash** (skin above and below), staff vertical in front
- activation: torso open to the enemy, lead arm straight at shoulder height with open palm 6–8 px ahead of the face, back arm pulled back, one leg raised (hop), hair/ribbons flung up and back
- spell FX drawn with no black outline: 4 steps of one hue (dark rim / mid / light / white), 1–10 px particles scattered 6–10 px around the body
